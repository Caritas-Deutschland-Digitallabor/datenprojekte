# Safer from Home to Work: AI for a better mobility infrastructure.

## Kurzbeschreibung
Inadequate infrastructure is a non-negligible factor in many traffic accidents. Using AI, satellite imagery, police accident reports and further data-sources, we propose to help municipalities pinpoint and decide in real time adaptations of infrastructures that will save lives.
