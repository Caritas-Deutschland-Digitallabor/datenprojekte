# IPSO - International Psychosocial Organisation

## Kurzbeschreibung
IPSO is a humanitarian nonprofit organization based in Germany specializing in Mental Health and Psychosocial Support Services (MHPSS) through digital worldwide accessible apps (ipso-care and ipso-saba) providing individual counseling to vulnerable individuals in 13 languages.
