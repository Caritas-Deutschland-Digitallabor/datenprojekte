# net4tec AI Career Coach

## Kurzbeschreibung
Accelerating gender diversity, leveraging full talent pool, enabling women to thrive in their careers is no longer "the right thing to do", but has become a necessity. The AI-Career Coach, powered chatbot offers visitors our website a clear, 5-point plan 4 advancing their careers
