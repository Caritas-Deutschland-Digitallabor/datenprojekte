# U Impact - Mobilising private capital towards sustainability in a human-centric approach

## Kurzbeschreibung
U Impact’s SaaS solution enhances sustainable fund inflows by gamifying the investor journey and using AI to deliver personalized ESG insights. We envision our AI-driven system to analyses behaviour, automates data integration, and refines recommendations to boost engagement.
